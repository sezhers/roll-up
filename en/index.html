Roll-up banner — advertising that travels with you

A roll-up is a retractable, free-standing advertising banner that can be set up in minutes and packed away just as quickly. It is one of the most common and cost-effective ways to bring your company's message to trade fairs, events, shops and the street — reusable, portable and always ready to use.

What is a roll-up and why does it work in advertising?

A roll-up consists of a printed banner attached to a retractable mechanism and an easy-to-carry base. That makes it a favourite for businesses that need advertising they can reuse at different events — trade fairs, presentations, openings, retail points or as part of an interior.

Unlike stickers or facade advertising, a roll-up needs no surface preparation or installation work — it is simply brought on site, unrolled and ready. That makes it a flexible addition to any advertising strategy, whether you have permanent signage or vehicle wraps.
